“From naught to crave, until craving the universe.”

I'm a second-year graduate student working in multimodal machine learning, where models learn from several kinds of input at once. My research is multimodal object detection (RGB-T), fusing visible and thermal images so a detector keeps working when one alone is not enough. I learn best by building and explaining, so I write code, take notes, and make slides — right now, working through Bishop's Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning, chapter by chapter.

Currently reading: Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction.
